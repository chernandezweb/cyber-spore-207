# CYBER-SPORE 207 // Boxhead Zombie Wars
> **2.5D Top-Down Action Roguelike Arena Shooter**  
> *Boxhead: The Zombie Wars* mechanics × *Risk of Rain 2* escalating difficulty loop & stacking relics × Cyber-Bioluminescent Alien World

---

## 🎮 Boxhead Gameplay Highlights

- **2.5D Top-Down Arena Perspective**: Full 8-way directional movement (`WASD`), 360° mouse aiming, and laser-sighted combat in tactical bunker rooms and corridors.
- **Iconic Boxhead Weapon Arsenal (`1 - 7` or Scroll Wheel)**:
  1. `[1] 🔫 DUAL PISTOLS`: Infinite ammo, rapid tap/hold, dependable sidearms.
  2. `[2] 💥 NEON SHOTGUN`: Heavy 6-pellet high-impact spread that pushes back zombie hordes.
  3. `[3] ⚡ CYBER-SMG`: Ultra high fire-rate pulse submachine gun to shred dense crowds.
  4. `[4] 💣 SPORE GRENADES`: Bouncing explosive bio-canisters that detonate on contact or timed fuse.
  5. `[5] 🚀 PLASMA ROCKETS`: Devastating long-range missile with massive shockwave detonation.
  6. `[6 or SPACE] 🛢️ EXPLOSIVE BARRELS`: Boxhead's signature mechanic! Drop red volatile barrels to create explosive traps. Shooting or damaging them triggers chain-reaction explosions across swarms!
  7. `[7 or F] 🛡️ CYBER BARRICADES`: Place energy barriers to construct chokepoints and fortify rooms against incoming swarms.
- **The Boxhead Combo Multiplier**:
  - Score continuous kills to escalate your combo meter: `x1 → x2 → x3 → x5 → x10 → x20`!
  - High combos boost your movement speed, weapon damage, and score.
- **Persistent Blood & Gore Splatters**:
  - Vaporized zombies leave permanent glowing alien blood/gore splatters across the arena floor.
- **Interactive Arena Features**:
  - **Mario Warp Pipes**: 4 Corner warp pipes on the floor — step into one to teleport diagonally across the arena to escape when cornered!
  - **Question Blocks `[?]`**: Shoot floating cubes to crack them open for ammo crates, 1-Up medkits, Starman invincibility, or RoR2 relics.
  - **Central Bio-Monolith Teleporter**: Activate with `[E]` to trigger the 40-second horde defense wave and slay **Bowser-Leviathan 207** to unlock the stage portal.
- **Risk of Rain 2 Synergies**:
  - Stacking relic items: Ukulele chain lightning, Soldier's Syringe fire rate, Brilliant Behemoth explosions, Fire Flower burn, and Tanooki feathers.
- **🤖 Built-in AI Auto-Pilot Tester**:
  - Press `[F4]` anytime to toggle autonomous AI testing (kites hordes, switches to shotguns/rockets, drops explosive barrels, and triggers warp pipes).

## 🕹️ Controls

| Key | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`W` `A` `S` `D`** | 8-Directional Top-Down Movement |
| **`Mouse Cursor`** | 360° Aiming |
| **`Left Mouse Button` / `J`** | Fire Active Weapon |
| **`1` to `7` or `Mouse Wheel`** | Select Weapon from Arsenal |
| **`Space`** | Quick-Place Explosive Barrel |
| **`F`** | Quick-Place Cyber Barricade |
| **`Shift` or `Right Mouse Button`** | Dash / Dodge Roll (Invulnerability frames) |
| **`R`** | Special Ultimate (Starman Overdrive / Tesla Singularity / Blade Storm) |
| **`E`** | Activate Bio-Monolith Teleporter / Next Stage |
| **`F4`** | Toggle AI Auto-Pilot Tester |
| **`M`** | Toggle Audio |
| **`Esc`** | Pause / Main Menu |
